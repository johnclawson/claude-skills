# Clawson Family Travel Planner

Points-optimized vacation planning for the Clawson family.

## The Family

**Points contributors:** John (primary planner), Rachel, Rich (brother-in-law)

**Travelers:** John, Rachel, Cole (14), Maggie (12), Grace (9), Levi (7) — sometimes +Rich = 7 total

## Planning Workflow

### Step 1: Inventory Check
Collect current points balances:
- Program name (Chase UR, Amex MR, Capital One, Bonvoy, Delta SkyMiles, etc.)
- Balance
- Card type (affects transfer partners)

### Step 2: Define the Trip
- **When:** Dates or flexible window
- **Who:** Group size (usually 6-7)
- **What:** Trip vibe — default toward history + outdoors
- **Budget:** Cash for expenses beyond points

### Step 3: Generate 2-3 Options
Rank by: (1) points value (cpp), (2) feasibility for group size, (3) experience fit

For each option:
- Flight redemption strategy (which program, transfer if needed)
- Accommodation: Airbnb preferred; hotel points acceptable
- Points cost per person and total
- Cash outlay estimate
- Why it's a good use of family points

### Step 4: Deep Dive
- Specific routing from SLC
- Award availability tips
- Transfer timing (instant vs delayed)
- Backup plans

## Key Preferences

**Flights:**
- Home airport: SLC (Delta hub)
- Preferred: Transfer points to Delta SkyMiles
- Best transfers: Amex MR → Delta (1:1), Amex MR → Virgin Atlantic (for Delta partner awards)
- Group size reality: 6-7 in business class is hard; be realistic about economy vs premium

**Accommodations:**
- Strong Airbnb preference (keeps group together)
- Hotel points okay for short trips or when vacation rentals are limited

**Ground:**
- Rent a car (default for group size)
- Skip rental only in major metros with excellent transit (NYC, London, Tokyo, Paris)

**Trip lengths:**
| Type | Duration |
|------|----------|
| Domestic city | 4-5 days |
| International | 7-10 days |
| UK+Ireland bucket list | 2-3 weeks |

**Activities:** Love history + outdoors (biking, beaches, hiking, cabins). Like museums/cities in moderation. Skip shopping.

## Scheduling Constraints

**Blackouts:** Halloween week, Thanksgiving week, Christmas/New Year's

**Flexibility:** Kids are homeschooled — very flexible outside blackouts (great for off-peak award availability)

**Fall semester:** John teaches at BYU on M/W. Can miss max 1-2 class days per semester.
- Best fall windows: Thu-Sun trips, BYU breaks, extended weekends with one M or W missed
- Spring/summer: no constraints

## Reminders

- Only transfer points you'll use (one-way transfers)
- Check cash prices — sometimes cash beats points
- Watch for Amex transfer bonuses (20-30% to Delta)
- Book early for 6-7 people — award space goes fast
- Virgin Atlantic often beats booking Delta direct for premium cabins
- For Florida cruises: fly MCO or FLL on points, cruise from Port Canaveral or Ft Lauderdale
