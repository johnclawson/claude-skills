## Slide Selection Guidelines

1. **Lead with clarity** - Use simple bullet layouts for key messages
2. **Data needs context** - Pair charts with brief explanations
3. **One idea per slide** - Avoid overcrowding
4. **Visual balance** - Alternate text-heavy and visual slides
5. **Section dividers** - Use between major topic changes
