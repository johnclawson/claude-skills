### High-Value Connection Routes

**Caribbean/Mexico:**
- SLC → ATL → Caribbean islands (Turks & Caicos, Aruba, St. Thomas)
- SLC → MSP → Cancun/Cozumel
- Puerto Rico (SLC → ATL → SJU) - El Morro fort, Old San Juan

**Europe (Delta One sweet spots via Virgin Atlantic booking):**
- SLC → JFK → London/Paris/Amsterdam
- SLC → ATL → Rome/Barcelona/Dublin
- SLC → SEA → Paris (seasonal)
- *Book Delta metal through Virgin Atlantic for better business class rates*

**Bucket List Europe Routings:**
- **London**: SLC → JFK/ATL → LHR
- **Scotland**: SLC → JFK → Edinburgh (EDI) or Glasgow (GLA), or via London
- **Ireland**: SLC → JFK/BOS → Dublin (DUB), or ATL → DUB
- **Denmark**: SLC → AMS → Copenhagen (CPH), or via London/Paris

**The Big UK + Ireland Trip (2-3 weeks):**
- Fly into London (LHR), out of Dublin (DUB) - or reverse
- Open-jaw booking saves backtracking
- Sample routing: London (4-5 days) → train to Edinburgh → Scotland (4-5 days) → fly to Dublin → Ireland (5-7 days) → home
- Mix of trains, rental car (Scotland Highlands, Ireland countryside), one internal flight

**Hawaii:**
- SLC → LAX/SEA → Maui (OGG), Kona (KOA), Kauai (LIH)
- Nonstop to HNL when available is best
- *Family has only done Oahu - Maui, Big Island, Kauai are fresh*

**Alaska:**
- SLC → SEA → Anchorage/Juneau/Fairbanks
